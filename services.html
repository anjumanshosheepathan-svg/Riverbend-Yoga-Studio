Classes and Services

Every class is taught with plain-language cues, optional variations, and permission to rest.

Class Types

  • Gentle Yoga

    A slow, supportive class built around seated, standing, and chair-supported poses. Poses are held longer so you have time to settle in.

    • Length: 60 minutes
    • Pace: slow, every pose described out loud
    • Props: blocks, straps, blankets, chairs

    Best for beginners, older adults, and students managing stiffness or fatigue.

  • Vinyasa

    A warming class that links breath to movement. Sequences stay short and repeat, so you learn the pattern instead of racing to keep up.

    • Length: 60 minutes
    • Pace: moderate, with a slower option shown
    • Props: blocks and blankets as needed

    Popular with busy professionals who want an efficient evening reset.

  • Restorative Yoga

    Five or six fully supported resting shapes held for several minutes each. Nothing is stretched aggressively; the goal is to help your nervous system downshift.

    • Length: 75 minutes
    • Pace: still and quiet, dim lighting
    • Props: bolsters, blankets, unscented eye pillows

    Best for stress relief and better sleep.

Private Sessions

One-to-one sessions are our most requested service for students who want extra reassurance before joining a group class. Your teacher builds a short home practice around your goals, your schedule, and any medical guidance you have received.

  • Single private session: 55 minutes, in studio or online
  • Three-session starter series: designed for brand-new students
  • Partner or caregiver session: practice alongside a family member

Private sessions are scheduled by request. Use the interest form on our Workshops & Events page and choose “Private session” as your interest.
