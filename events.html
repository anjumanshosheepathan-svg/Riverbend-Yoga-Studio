Workshops and Events

Workshops are capped at twelve students, so there is time for questions.

Requesting a spot does not commit you to payment — we will confirm availability first and answer any questions before you decide.

This Month's Workshops

  • Yoga Basics: Your First Four Poses

    Saturday, March 7 · 10:00–11:30 a.m.

    A no-pressure introduction to standing, seated, and reclining shapes, plus how to use blocks and straps. No experience needed.

  • Rest & Restore

    Friday, March 13 · 6:30–8:00 p.m.

    Six supported resting poses held for several minutes each, with dim lighting and optional unscented eye pillows.

  • Yoga at Your Desk

    Wednesday, March 18 · 12:00–12:45 p.m.

    A lunchtime session of seated shoulder, neck, and wrist relief you can repeat at work. Street clothes welcome.

  • Chair Yoga for Strength and Balance

    Tuesday, March 24 · 9:30–10:45 a.m.

    Designed with older adults in mind, this workshop builds steadiness using a chair for support throughout.

  • Breath and Sleep

    Sunday, March 29 · 4:00–5:15 p.m.

    Learn three simple breathing practices, including the one featured on our home page, to use at bedtime.
