Welcome to Riverbend Yoga Studio

Featured Class: Gentle Flow for Beginners

Your first class is always free, and props are always encouraged.

Gentle Flow meets every Tuesday and Saturday morning. Movements are linked slowly to the breath, transitions are demonstrated twice, and every pose is offered in a standing, seated, or chair-supported version. Most students describe it as the calmest hour of their week.

Best for: first-time students, older adults, and anyone returning from a long break.

Our Mission

Riverbend Yoga Studio exists to make yoga feel accessible, unhurried, and welcoming for every body in our neighborhood. We teach slow, clearly explained classes where props are encouraged, modifications are offered out loud, and no one is asked to keep up with anyone else.

Whether you are stepping onto a mat for the very first time, easing back after an injury, or looking for a calm reset between work meetings, there is a place for you here.

New to Yoga? Start Here

You do not need flexibility, special clothing, or previous experience to join us.

  • 1. Arrive early

    Come about ten minutes before class and let the front desk know it is your first visit.

  • 2. Borrow props

    A mat, two blocks, and a strap are yours at no charge, and we will show you how to use them.

  • 3. Tell your teacher

    Share any injuries or concerns so your teacher can adapt poses for you before class starts.

  • 4. Rest anytime

    Choosing to pause is always an accepted option in our rooms. No explanation needed.

Ready to plan a visit? Review our class types or request a spot in an upcoming workshop.

Try a One-Minute Guided Breathing Practice

Curious what our teaching voice sounds like before you visit? Press play for a short guided breathing exercise you can do seated at your desk.

Written version of the breathing practice

Sit tall and let your shoulders soften. Breathe in slowly through your nose for a count of four. Pause gently for a count of two. Breathe out through your nose for a count of six. Repeat this cycle five times, and notice how your jaw, neck, and hands feel afterward.
