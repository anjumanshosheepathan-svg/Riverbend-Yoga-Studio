About Riverbend Yoga Studio

Yoga should adapt to the student, not the other way around.

Riverbend Yoga Studio opened in 2019 in a former print shop two blocks from the river trail. Our three teachers share one belief: every body deserves a class that meets it where it is.

Meet Our Instructors

  • Maya Chen, Lead Instructor

    Maya holds a 500-hour certification plus training in chair and adaptive yoga. She teaches Gentle Flow and Chair & Gentle Yoga and describes every pose out loud so students never have to look up to follow along.

  • Daniel Ortiz, Vinyasa Instructor

    Daniel came to yoga after fifteen years of desk work and back pain. He teaches Slow Vinyasa and Level 1–2 Vinyasa and always offers a lower-intensity version of each sequence.

  • Priya Raman, Restorative Instructor

    Priya leads Candlelight Restorative and our monthly rest workshops. She trained in trauma-informed teaching and offers hands-on assists only when a student asks for them.

Accessibility Accommodations

We want you to know what to expect before you arrive. Tell us what would make your practice easier — you never need to explain a diagnosis.

  • Step-free entrance from the sidewalk and a wide interior doorway
  • Accessible restroom and a bench-seating changing area
  • Chairs available in every class, including Vinyasa
  • All poses cued verbally, with a demonstration repeated on request
  • Unscented eye pillows and a fragrance-free studio policy
  • Dimmable lighting and volume-adjustable music, or silence on request
  • Large-print class schedules available at the front desk

Studio Policies

Arrival
Doors open 20 minutes before class. New students are asked to arrive 10 minutes early.
Cancellations
Cancel a reserved spot up to two hours before class at no charge by phone or email.
Props and mats
Mats, blocks, straps, blankets, and bolsters are provided at no additional cost.
Quiet space
The practice room is a phone-free space so classes stay calm and focused.
Weather closures
Closures are announced by email and on the studio voicemail at least 90 minutes ahead.

Questions about a policy or an accommodation? Send them through the interest form on our Workshops & Events page and we will reply within one business day.
